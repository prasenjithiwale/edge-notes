# Contributing

Ledge is open source and **not open to contributions**. It is one person's app,
built to one set of opinions about what it should be, and keeping it that way is
most of why it stays small.

- **Bug reports are welcome.** [Open an issue](https://github.com/prasenjithiwale/edge-notes/issues/new/choose)
  with what happened, what you expected, and the details from
  Settings › About › Copy (version and system).
- **Feature requests** are not taken as a queue. If something is missing, a bug
  report describing what you were trying to do is still useful.
- **Pull requests** are not merged as a rule. Every change to `master` needs the
  owner's review, and a pull request may be closed without one.
- **Security problems:** please do not open a public issue. See
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

You are free to fork it under the [MIT licence](LICENSE).
